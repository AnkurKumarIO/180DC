VISHLESHAN Details

Vishleshan — Flagship Case Competition

Vishleshan is our flagship case competition — the one event on campus where business problems don't come with textbook answers. Participants get a real-world case, a limited window to work through it, and a panel of judges who won't let vague reasoning slide. It's competitive, fast, and a solid indicator of who can actually think on their feet. Now in its second edition at VNIT, Vishleshan has grown into one of the institute's go-to consulting events, drawing participants from across campus who want to test their problem solving against peers.

ESCAPE ROOM Details

Escape Room

Escape Room was built around a simple idea — if you can't diagnose a problem correctly, your solution won't matter. The event ran across three elimination rounds. Round one was guesstimates: participants had to estimate the answer to a given problem, and only those within an acceptable range advanced. Round two asked them to do a root cause analysis on a new problem — not just identify what's wrong, but why. Round three closed the loop: teams had to design a product based on the root cause they had identified in the previous round, with judges evaluating both the logic and the output. It was one of our more unconventional events, and one that people came out of talking about.

ISB Session Details

ISB Session

We brought in a speaker from the Indian School of Business for a campus session focused on one thing: what it actually takes to build a competitive profile. Students heard directly about how ISB evaluates candidates, what distinguishes strong applicants, and what the Young Leaders programme looks like from the inside. It was a candid conversation — no vague advice about following your passion, just practical guidance on how to position yourself well. For anyone thinking about business school or top-tier post-grad programmes, it was a useful few hours.
