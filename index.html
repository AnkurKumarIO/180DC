180 Degrees Consulting

180 Degrees Consulting is the world’s largest university based consultancy for non-profits and social enterprises. We connect ambitious, socially conscious university students with organizations that are creating positive impact across the globe. By combining fresh perspectives, analytical thinking, and a passion for social change, we help organizations overcome challenges, strengthen their strategies, and maximize their impact, all while making high quality consulting more accessible.

VNIT Nagpur

Established in 1960, Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur is one of India’s premier engineering and research institutions, recognized for its academic excellence, innovation, and dynamic student community. With a strong culture of leadership, collaboration, and problem-solving, VNIT empowers students to create meaningful impact both within and beyond the campus.
